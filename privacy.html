Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Atlas Backups is a personal backup utility used by its owner.

Google Drive access

The utility requests permission to create, read, and manage Google Drive files and folders created through the application. It uses this access to upload and verify Atlas backups and download them for recovery.

Data storage and sharing

Backups may contain private portfolio and financial research data. They are stored on the owner's computer and in the owner's Google Drive. The utility does not sell this data, use it for advertising, or intentionally share it with other parties. Google processes uploaded files under its own terms and privacy policy.

Credentials

Google authorization tokens are stored locally on the backup computer and are excluded from backup uploads.

Retention and deletion

Backups remain until the owner deletes them or configures a retention policy. The owner can revoke the application's access through their Google Account settings. Revoking access does not delete existing backups.
